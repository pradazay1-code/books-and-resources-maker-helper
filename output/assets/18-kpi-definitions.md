> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault

**Chapter 14 · Stage 6 — Proof**

What to track, how to calculate it, and what it tells you. **No target ranges** — the only benchmark worth anything is your own last period.

### Your business

| Metric | Formula | What it tells you | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attempts | Count of outbound touches | Whether you're doing the work | 2 |
| Reply rate | Replies ÷ attempts | Message and targeting quality | 2 |
| Conversation rate | Convos ÷ attempts | Whether the interest is real | 2 |
| Stage conversion | Stage N+1 ÷ Stage N | Exactly where the funnel breaks | 7 |
| Show rate (your calls) | Calls held ÷ calls booked | Gap between booking and the call | 9 |
| Close rate | Deals ÷ calls held | Call quality — and whether price is low | 9 |
| Call-to-close | Deals ÷ calls booked | The honest end-to-end number | 9 |
| Attempts to first yes | Count, once | Your entry fee. Compare everything to it | 1 |
| Monthly churn | Lost ÷ opening client count | Whether you're running to stand still | 15 |
| Client lifetime (months) | 1 ÷ monthly churn | What a client is actually worth | 15 |
| Referrals per client | Introductions ÷ active clients | Whether the domino is running | 16 |
| Close rate: referred vs cold | Two rates, compared | The cash value of delivery quality | 16 |

### Why no targets

A benchmark from someone else's market is worse than no benchmark. A reply rate that's poor in one vertical is excellent in another, and a reader who quits at 6% because a book said 12% has been actively misled.

**Measure your own for 30 days. That's your baseline. Then beat it.**

### The three that matter most

1. **Days to first booked appointment** — predicts retention
2. **Stage-to-stage conversion** — tells you what to fix
3. **Referrals per client** — tells you whether the business compounds
