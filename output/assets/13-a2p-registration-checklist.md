# A2P 10DLC Registration Checklist
> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault

**Chapter 11 · Stage 5 — Build**

> **Accurate as of August 2026.** Carrier requirements, fees, and timelines change. Verify with your provider. The sequencing advice — register first — does not change.

**Submit on day one.** This runs on carrier time, not yours. It's the only part of the build you cannot compress.

### Before you open the form

- [ ] Legal business name, **exactly** as registered with the IRS — not the trade name, not the name on the truck
- [ ] EIN, and the address on the EIN filing
- [ ] Business type and industry
- [ ] Website live, and describing the business the campaign describes
- [ ] **Privacy policy that specifically mentions SMS**
- [ ] Privacy policy states mobile data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing
- [ ] Opt-in consent language visible **next to the submit button** — not buried in terms
- [ ] Screenshot of the opt-in saved
- [ ] Contact name, email on the business domain, phone
- [ ] Sample messages written — the actual texts you'll send, placeholders filled

### Costs — approximate, August 2026

| Item | Cost | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Brand — sole proprietor | ~$4 | One-time |
| Brand — standard, with vetting | ~$48+ | One-time |
| Campaign vetting | ~$15–17 | Per campaign |
| Campaign monthly | ~$1.50–$10 | Monthly, varies by use case |
| Carrier surcharges | ~$0.003–$0.005 | Per message segment |

Put this in your setup fee so it's never a line-item conversation.

### The three common rejection causes

1. Legal name doesn't match IRS records
2. Website has no SMS clause in the privacy policy
3. Sample messages don't match the stated use case

### Say it on the sales call

> "First thing I do is get your messaging registered with the carriers. Takes about a week and it's out of my hands — but if we don't do it first, none of your texts get delivered. Most people find that out three weeks in."

Sets the expectation before it becomes a complaint, and shows you know something the last agency didn't.

> ⚠️ **Registration is not consent.** Carriers agreeing to deliver your traffic has nothing to do with whether you had permission to send it. See asset 21.
