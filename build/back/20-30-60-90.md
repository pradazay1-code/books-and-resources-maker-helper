## The 30/60/90 Execution Plan

The whole book as a calendar. If you do nothing else, do this in this order.

### Days 1–30 · Get to your first conversation

The goal of month one is not a client. It's a working outreach habit and a real pipeline.

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Write your attempt-number commitment and the date <em>(Ch. 1)</em></li>
<li>Find your daily block and set the recurring calendar event <em>(Ch. 2)</em></li>
<li>Score three verticals, pick one, draw the radius <em>(Ch. 3)</em></li>
<li>Count the qualifying businesses inside it <em>(Ch. 3)</em></li>
<li>Fill the offer builder and say the one-sentence version out loud ten times <em>(Ch. 4)</em></li>
<li>Do the job-value arithmetic for one real business <em>(Ch. 5)</em></li>
<li>Set your setup fee and monthly <em>(Ch. 5)</em></li>
<li>Book the attorney review of your MSA and SOW <em>(Ch. 6)</em></li>
<li>Build the six-stage outreach pipeline <em>(Ch. 7)</em></li>
<li>Add 200+ businesses to Cold <em>(Ch. 7)</em></li>
<li>Write your four-part message for two channels <em>(Ch. 8)</em></li>
<li>Start your own A2P 10DLC brand registration <em>(Ch. 11)</em></li>
<li><strong>Hit your daily number every single day</strong></li>
</ul>

### Days 31–60 · Get to your first client

Month two is conversations. Your outreach is running; now the skill is on the call.

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Write and rehearse your opening frame <em>(Ch. 9)</em></li>
<li>Build your fifteen-question discovery sheet <em>(Ch. 9)</em></li>
<li>Write your answer to all twelve objections and say each out loud <em>(Ch. 10)</em></li>
<li>Run calls. Tally which objections actually appear <em>(Ch. 10)</em></li>
<li>Review stage-to-stage conversion weekly; fix the biggest drop-off <em>(Ch. 7)</em></li>
<li>Build the client-side pipeline and the speed-to-lead automation <em>(Ch. 12)</em></li>
<li>Test the whole path as a fake lead from your own phone <em>(Ch. 12)</em></li>
<li>Write your day 0–14 onboarding checklist <em>(Ch. 13)</em></li>
<li>Audit every document for income claims <em>(Ch. 6, 17)</em></li>
<li><strong>Keep hitting your daily number, including on call days</strong></li>
</ul>

### Days 61–90 · Deliver, prove, and tip the next one

Month three is where the domino either starts or doesn't. It starts with delivery.

<ul class="checklist">
<li>Submit client A2P registration within one business day of signature <em>(Ch. 11)</em></li>
<li>Send a progress note every 2–3 days during the registration wait <em>(Ch. 13)</em></li>
<li>Get real calendar availability in writing <em>(Ch. 13)</em></li>
<li>Build the reminder stack <em>(Ch. 13)</em></li>
<li>Call the client the day the first appointment books <em>(Ch. 13)</em></li>
<li>Build the one-page report and send it on time <em>(Ch. 14)</em></li>
<li>Set the two-week check-in as a recurring task <em>(Ch. 15)</em></li>
<li>Ask for a referral the day a job is reported won <em>(Ch. 16)</em></li>
<li>Get written permission before using any result publicly <em>(Ch. 16, 17)</em></li>
<li>Snapshot the build so the next one takes hours <em>(Ch. 12)</em></li>
<li>Start your mistake list <em>(Ch. 18)</em></li>
<li><strong>Still hitting the daily number</strong></li>
</ul>

<div class="pull">If you notice one instruction repeating in all three months, that's not an editing error. It's the book.</div>
