## Glossary

**A2P 10DLC** — Application-to-Person messaging over a 10-digit long code. What your CRM does when it sends a text. Requires carrier registration; unregistered traffic is blocked.

**Activation point** — The specific thing a customer does or receives that separates the ones who stay from the ones who leave. For a lead-gen agency, most likely the client's first booked appointment.

**BAMFAM** — Book a meeting from a meeting. Never end a call without the next one scheduled.

**Brand (A2P)** — The registered business identity behind messaging. Legal name, EIN, address. One per business.

**CAC** — Customer acquisition cost. What it costs you, in money or hours, to land one client.

**Campaign (A2P)** — A registered messaging use case under a brand, with sample messages and an opt-in description. A brand can have several.

**Churn** — Customers lost in a period, divided by the customers you started that period with. New signups during the period don't change it.

**Cold** — A prospect on your list who has never been contacted. The first stage of the outreach pipeline.

**Consent (TCPA)** — The recipient's permission to be messaged. Separate from carrier registration, which only governs delivery.

**Lookback window** — The period a client actually judges you over, which is the last billing cycle rather than the whole relationship.

**LTV** — Lifetime value. What a client is worth across the relationship. Roughly monthly revenue divided by monthly churn.

**MSA** — Master Services Agreement. The rules of the relationship, signed once.

**Show rate** — Booked appointments that turned up, as a percentage of appointments booked.

**Snapshot** — A saved template of a fully built client account, used so the next build starts from something rather than nothing.

**SOW** — Statement of Work. What you're doing, for how much, over what period. One per engagement.

**Speed to lead** — Elapsed time between an inquiry arriving and your first contact attempt. Measured in seconds.

**Structural churn** — Clients lost because their business closed, rather than because of anything you did. Track it separately.

**Throughput** — The percentage of engaged leads who end up showing to an appointment. Schedule rate multiplied by show rate.

**Usage churn** — A client who has stopped using the product but is still paying. A leading indicator of actual churn.
