### Rules

**Lead with appointments, not impressions.** Platform metrics are your diagnostic, not his report. Keep them in your own view.

**"Jobs won (you told us)" is labeled deliberately.** You can't verify what he closed. Labeling it as reported keeps you accurate — and gives you a monthly reason to ask, which is the number you need for case studies later.

**Send it in a bad month, on time, with the bad number first.** A missing report reads as hiding.

### The bad month message

```
"August was down — 6 booked against 11 in July.

Here's what I think happened: [specific diagnosis, not
'the algorithm changed'].

Here's what I'm changing: [one or two specific things,
with dates].

Here's what I need from you: [something concrete, or
'nothing, this one's on me'].

I'll have the first read on whether it's working by [date]."
```

### Weekly two-liner

Between monthly reports, thirty seconds:

```
"{{N}} booked this week. {{one thing that needs him, or
'nothing needed from you'}}."
```

Keeps the lookback window continuously full.
