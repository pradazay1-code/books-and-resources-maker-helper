# Offer Builder Worksheet
> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault
> Fill this in. Do not retype it out of the book.

**Chapter 4 · Stage 2 — Offer**

Seven fields. The output is one sentence you can say without flinching.

### 1. Who

I sell to ________________________ within ______ minutes of ________________________.

### 2. The unit

I deliver: ________________________________________________

Defined precisely as:

> _A scheduled appointment with ______________________ [decision-maker], located in ______________________ [service area], who has confirmed the time by ______________________ [text / phone / email]._

Write this definition into the SOW. It is the argument you're not having in month three.

### 3. The outcome, in their units

______ [appointments] in the first ______ days.

Not "more customers." A number and a timeframe, in the units they already think in.

### 4. What one unit is worth to them

- Average job value: $________
- Their close rate on estimates: ______%
- **Value of one appointment: $________**

### 5. Time to first result

- A2P registration submitted: day ______ (should be day 1)
- Registration approved: day ______ (carrier time — 4 to 15 days)
- Build complete: day ______
- **First appointment expected by: day ______**

### Language check before you send anything

- [ ] No income claim. Describe the service, never predict their earnings.
- [ ] No "guarantee," "will make," "ROI," or "promise."
- [ ] The unit is defined precisely enough to be measured.
