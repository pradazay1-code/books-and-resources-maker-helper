### Step 3 — Split the fee

| | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|

### Step 4 — Three tiers

| | Starter | Standard | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup (one-time) | $______ | $______ | $______ |

| **Best for** | Solo operator | 2–5 crew | Multi-crew / multi-location |

That last row does more work than the prices. Name the buyer, not the feature.

### Step 5 — The signals you're too cheap

- [ ] Closing more than half the people who hear the price
- [ ] At capacity and can't take another client
- [ ] Never lost a deal on price

Any box ticked means you haven't found your ceiling yet.
