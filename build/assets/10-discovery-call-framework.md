# Discovery Call Framework
> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault
> Fill this in. Do not retype it out of the book.

**Chapter 9 · Stage 4 — Close**

Diagnose before you prescribe. The person asking the questions is running the call.

### Before — five minutes

- [ ] Their site, reviews, social
- [ ] Is there an obvious way for a customer to request a quote?
- [ ] Write down **two things you noticed** and **one guess** about their constraint

### The six moves

**1 · Frame it (30 seconds)**

```
"Thanks for making time. Quick heads up, I record these so I
can go back through my notes — that alright with you?

Here's how I run these. About twenty minutes. I'm going to ask
you a bunch of questions about how you're getting work right
now, because I don't know yet whether I can help you.

At the end, one of two things happens. Either I think I can
help and I'll tell you exactly what that looks like and what
it costs — or I don't, and I'll tell you that too and point
you somewhere better. Either way you'll know today.

Sound good?"
```

> **Massachusetts:** M.G.L. c. 272 § 99 makes it a felony to *secretly* record. Announce it, get the acknowledgment. If you'd rather not deal with it, don't record — but don't record quietly.

**2 · Current state** — how work actually arrives today. Specifics, not categories.

**3 · The gap** — what they want that isn't happening. Quantify it.

**4 · What they've tried** — the most valuable section, and the one most people skip.

**5 · Cost of doing nothing** — let *him* say it. If you say it, it's a pitch.

**6 · Prescribe, price, ask** — in that order, and not before.

### The close

```
"So based on what you told me — [restate his gap in HIS words]
— here's what I'd do. [The offer, thirty seconds.]

That's [setup] to build it and [monthly] to run it.

Do you want to do it?"
```

**Then stop talking.** Let it be uncomfortable. If you fill the silence you'll fill it with a discount.

### If it's not a yes today

Do not end on "I'll follow up." Open the calendar while you're both live and book the next thing. Somebody being vague about the next step is an objection, not a scheduling problem.

### After every call

- [ ] Which objections came up? (tally them — asset 12)
- [ ] Next meeting booked, or a clear no
- [ ] Notes in the CRM within 10 minutes, while it's fresh
