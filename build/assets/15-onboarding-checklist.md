# Client Onboarding Checklist — Day 0 to 14
> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault
> Fill this in. Do not retype it out of the book.

**Chapter 13 · Stage 5 → 6**

Everything here is bent toward one thing: **the client's first booked appointment.** That's the activation point, and how fast you reach it predicts whether they stay.

### Day 0 — signature day

- [ ] Setup fee collected
- [ ] **A2P registration submitted the same day** (asset 13)
- [ ] Onboarding call booked before the signature call ends
- [ ] Access request sent

### Day 1 — onboarding call

- [ ] Collect access: ad accounts, domain, existing CRM, phone
- [ ] **Get real calendar availability in writing** — actual windows, including some evenings. "Whenever" books nothing
- [ ] Resell the outcome in his words
- [ ] Walk through exactly what happens over 14 days — **including the registration wait and why it exists**
- [ ] Tell him: "If you ever want to stop, I just ask for fifteen minutes first"
- [ ] Book the day-14 review now

### Days 2–4 — building

- [ ] Build per asset 14
- [ ] **Send one short progress note even though nothing is live**

> Silence in week one is what kills month two. From his side, "building" and "nothing happening" look identical.

### Day 5 — test

- [ ] Full path tested as a fake lead, from your own phone on cellular
- [ ] **Show the client the test.** Seeing it work before it's live buys you the rest of the wait

### Day 7+ — live

- [ ] Registration approved
- [ ] Campaigns on
- [ ] **Tell him the hour it went live**

### First appointment — whenever it lands

- [ ] **Call him the day it happens.** Not email. Not the weekly report. Call
- [ ] "You've got a real one Thursday at two. Here's what they asked about."
- [ ] Log days from signature → first booked appointment: **______**

### Day 14 — first review

- [ ] What came in, what booked, what showed
- [ ] One thing you're changing
- [ ] One thing you need from him
- [ ] Next check-in scheduled

### The two nobody does

- [ ] Progress note on day 3 with nothing live
- [ ] Phone call the day the first appointment books

Cheapest retention work in the whole business.
