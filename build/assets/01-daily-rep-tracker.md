### Definitions, so you don't drift

- **Attempt** — one outbound touch to one business. A follow-up counts.
- **Reply** — any response, including "not interested." Rude ones count.
- **Conversation** — they engaged past the first exchange.

### The rules

1. Attempts is the only number you control. It's the only one that's a target.
2. Set the daily number for your worst realistic day, not your best.
3. Do not change your message, offer, or vertical before 300 attempts.
4. Total it every Sunday. Four minutes.
