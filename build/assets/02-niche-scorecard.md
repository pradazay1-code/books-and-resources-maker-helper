### How to score the two that matter most

**Owner reachable directly (×3).** 5 = the owner answers the phone. 3 = a small office, one gatekeeper. 1 = corporate, a marketing manager, a vendor list.

**One extra job covers your fee (×3).** Do the arithmetic:

- Average job value: $________
- Their close rate on estimates: ______%
- **Value of one appointment = job value × close rate = $________**

5 = one appointment is worth several times your monthly fee. 3 = roughly comparable. 1 = you'd need a spreadsheet to justify it — pick a different vertical.
