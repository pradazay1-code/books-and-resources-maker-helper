**Chapter 8 · Stage 3 — Volume**

> ⚠️ **These are structures, not proven copy.** Fill them with your own words and your own specifics. A message you can't say out loud in your own voice will not survive contact with a real business owner.

### The four-part anatomy

Every message that gets answered does the same four things in the same order.

1. **Proof you looked** — one specific, checkable detail. Does 80% of the work.
2. **The observation** — one sentence connecting that detail to a problem or a window. Not a pitch.
3. **What you do** — one line, in their units. Booked estimates. Not "marketing."
4. **A tiny ask** — a yes/no question that costs nothing to answer.

Two to four sentences total. The goal of the first message is a **reply**, not a call.

---

### DM

```
[SPECIFIC DETAIL — visible only by actually looking at their
page, truck, site, or listing]

[ONE-LINE OBSERVATION connecting it to a problem or an
opportunity window. No pitch.]

[WHAT YOU DO, in their units.]

[TINY ASK — yes/no.]
```

No greeting block. No signature. Write like a person texting.

---

### Email

```
subject: [lowercase, specific to them, no pitch]

[SPECIFIC DETAIL.]

[OBSERVATION — one or two sentences.]

[WHAT YOU DO, one line, in their units.]

[TINY ASK.]

[First name]
```

Keep the body short enough to read on a phone without scrolling.

---

### Cold call opener

```
"Hey [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] — I'm local, over in [TOWN].
This is a cold call, so I'll be quick.

[SPECIFIC DETAIL + OBSERVATION, one breath.]

[WHAT YOU DO, one line.]

Is that something you'd want to hear about, or should I
leave you alone?"
```

Naming it as a cold call disarms what they're already thinking. The explicit exit gets you a real answer instead of a polite dodge — and a fast no is a cheap no.

---

### Walk-in

Your biggest edge, and almost nobody does it. An agency three time zones away cannot walk into a landscaping office on a Tuesday morning.

- Go at a slow hour for their trade
- Ask for the owner **by name** — which means you looked him up, which is step one already done
- Say the four-part message
- Leave one page
- Get out. Three minutes.

```
"Morning — is [OWNER NAME] around?

[SPECIFIC DETAIL.] [OBSERVATION.]

[WHAT YOU DO, one line.]

I'll leave this with you — worth a conversation?"
```

### Before you send

- [ ] First line is about **them**, not you
- [ ] The specific detail could not be sent to any other business
- [ ] The ask is yes/no, not a request for 15 minutes
- [ ] You said it out loud and it sounded like a person
- [ ] SMS only after A2P 10DLC registration (asset 13)
- [ ] Same message, unchanged, for 300 attempts before you judge it
