# Discovery Question Bank
> **From The OneVision Playbook** by Isaiah Wright · onevision asset vault
> Fill this in. Do not retype it out of the book.

**Chapter 9 · Stage 4 — Close**

Pick fifteen. Put them in order. Print it and keep it next to you — reading questions off a page is fine and nobody can tell.

### Current state

- How do people find you right now?
- Where did your last five jobs come from?
- How many estimates do you go out on in a normal week?
- Out of ten estimates, how many turn into work?
- What's an average job worth to you?
- Who answers the phone when someone calls?
- What happens if someone fills out your form at 8pm?
- What happens if you're on a job and the phone rings?

### The gap

- How many more jobs a week could you actually handle?
- What's stopping you from being there now?
- What does your calendar look like eight weeks out?
- When's your busy season? When does the phone go quiet?
- What kind of job do you *want* more of?

### What they've tried

- Have you worked with a marketing company before?
- What happened?
- What did you like about it? What made you stop?
- Did anyone ever tell you what was actually happening?
- What are you doing yourself right now to get work?
- What's the last thing you tried that didn't work?

### Cost of doing nothing

- If it's the same twelve months from now, what does that cost you?
- Have you had to turn work down?
- Have you had to let anyone go in a slow stretch?
- What would three more jobs a month change for you?

### Decision

- If we did this, who else is in on the decision?
- What would need to be true for you to say yes today?
- What's your gut telling you right now?

---

**Ask "who else is in on the decision" at minute fifteen**, not at the end. It saves you the call where a partner appears out of nowhere.

**Always ask what they've tried before.** A prospect burned by a previous agency has objections you can address before they surface — and you only find out by asking.
