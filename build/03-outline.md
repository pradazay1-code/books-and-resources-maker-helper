# 03 — Book Architecture

**Phase 3 output.** Target: 28,000–32,000 words · 130–150 designed pages · 8.5×11 trim.

**Title:** *The OneVision Playbook*
**Subtitle:** *How I Built a Local Marketing Agency From a Dorm Room — and the System You Can Run Yourself*
**Author:** Isaiah Wright

---

## The spine

The book runs on **The Domino System** — seven stages, looping. Every chapter belongs to a
stage, and the reader is told which one at the top of the page.

```
LANE → OFFER → VOLUME → CLOSE → BUILD → PROOF → TIP
  ↑                                                │
  └────────── each client tips the next ───────────┘
```

**The promise, stated once and kept:** the first client is the hardest thing you will do. Each
one after costs less. This book gets you to the first one and shows you how the rest compound.

**What it refuses to promise:** a timeline, an income figure, or that it will be fast.

---

## Story slot convention

Cold opens marked `[STORY NEEDED: ...]` are Isaiah's to fill. Each carries a specific prompt
naming the exact moment required — not "add a story here," but "the day you got the first yes,
where you were standing, what they said." Chapters are written so the surrounding teaching
stands on its own and the story slots into a shaped hole.

**Chapters are graded for how much they depend on unsupplied material:**

- 🟢 **Ships now** — teaching stands complete; the story slot is enrichment
- 🟡 **Ships with a visible hole** — usable, but noticeably thinner without his material
- 🔴 **Cannot ship** — the chapter *is* the story; without it there's nothing to publish
