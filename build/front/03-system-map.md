<div class="legend">
<div><b>1 · LANE</b> — Choose one vertical and one radius you can physically drive to. Written down, today.</div>
<div><b>2 · OFFER</b> — Build one offer at one price, sold in booked appointments, not vague "leads."</div>
<div><b>3 · VOLUME</b> — A daily touch count you hit whether or not you feel like it, tracked in a pipeline.</div>
<div><b>4 · CLOSE</b> — Diagnose before you pitch. End the call with a decision, not a "let me think."</div>
<div><b>5 · BUILD</b> — Register for messaging first, then stand up the machine. Drive hard at the activation point.</div>
<div><b>6 · PROOF</b> — Report in the client's language: appointments and jobs won, not impressions.</div>
<div><b>7 · TIP</b> — Ask at the moment the result lands. The referral re-enters at VOLUME, already half closed.</div>
</div>

<p class="attrib">Stages 1 and 2 you do once and revisit. Stages 3 through 7 are the loop you live in. The dotted line is the whole point of this book: work done at stage 7 shows up as easier work at stage 3, which is why the tenth client costs a fraction of what the first one did.</p>
